ReadMe — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

1. Introduction

ReadMe (“the App”) is a reading assistance application that helps users listen to written content using text-to-speech technology. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use its features.

2. Information processed by the App

Depending on the features you use, ReadMe may process:

Documents and text: Text from files you select, including supported text documents and PDFs.

On-screen content: Text from other apps or visible screen content when you explicitly activate ReadMe’s accessibility-based reading or screen-capture features and grant the required permissions.

Reading settings: Preferences such as speech voice, volume, speed and pitch.

Reading progress: The position or progress information needed to resume reading, where supported.

The App is designed to process reading content on your device. Reading content and screen text may be sensitive, so only activate the relevant features and permissions when you intend to use them.

3. How information is used

Information processed by ReadMe is used to provide reading assistance, convert text into speech, support reading controls and retain applicable settings or reading progress.

Text-to-speech processing may be performed by the speech engine installed on your device. The handling of speech data by that engine is subject to the engine provider’s own privacy practices.

4. Data collection and sharing

ReadMe does not require users to create an account to use its reading features, and the current version does not display advertisements.

The developer should verify the behaviour of the distributed app and all included third-party libraries before making a final declaration about data collection or sharing. Any information transmitted by the App or its libraries, if applicable, must be disclosed accurately.

5. Storage and retention

App settings and reading-progress information may be stored locally on your device to support the App’s features. Files you select remain subject to the Android storage and document-access mechanisms.

You can manage permissions through Android settings. Uninstalling the App generally removes its private local app data, subject to Android and device-specific behaviour. Files stored elsewhere on your device may remain.

6. Permissions

Some features require permissions or special access, which may include:

Accessibility access: To support reading content from other apps when you enable this feature.

Screen capture or related access: If required by a supported screen-reading feature.

Document access: To open files that you choose to read.

These permissions should be used only for the purposes described in the App. You can revoke permissions through your device settings, although some features may stop working as a result.

7. Children's privacy

ReadMe is not specifically designed for children. The developer should ensure that the App’s actual intended audience and Google Play target-audience declaration are consistent with this statement.

8. Security

The developer aims to minimise the information processed by the App and to use appropriate safeguards. No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s features, permissions or data practices change. The latest version should be published at the same public URL, with its effective date updated when appropriate.
